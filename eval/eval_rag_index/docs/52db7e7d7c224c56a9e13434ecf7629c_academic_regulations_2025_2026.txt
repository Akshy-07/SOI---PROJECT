COLLEGE ACADEMIC REGULATIONS 2025-2026

SECTION 1. ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS
1.1 Every student is required to attend a minimum of 75% of scheduled lectures, practicals, and tutorial sessions in each registered course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations.
1.2 Students securing attendance between 65% and 74% due to medical reasons or sanctioned On-Duty (OD) participation in recognized institutional events may apply for condonation. Condonation requires a verified medical certificate and payment of a condonation fee of Rs. 1,000 per subject, subject to approval by the Academic Council.
1.3 Students with attendance below 65% shall not be permitted to write the end-semester examinations under any circumstances. Such students will be declared detained in that semester and must re-register for the courses when offered in the subsequent academic year.
1.4 On-Duty (OD) requests must be submitted at least 2 working days prior to the event and approved by the Head of Department (HOD) to be credited towards overall attendance.

SECTION 2. INTERNAL ASSESSMENTS AND GRADING
2.1 Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) accounts for 40% of the total course evaluation, comprising Internal Assessment 1 (50 marks), Internal Assessment 2 (50 marks), and Assignments/Quizzes (10 marks), aggregated to 110 marks and scaled.
2.2 The minimum pass mark for internal assessment is 50% in continuous evaluation.
2.3 Re-test for internal assessments is conducted solely for students who missed examinations on approved institutional On-Duty or verified hospitalization.
2.4 Letter grades follow the 10-point scale: O (91-100%, Outstanding, Grade Point 10), A+ (81-90%, Excellent, Grade Point 9), A (71-80%, Very Good, Grade Point 8), B+ (61-70%, Good, Grade Point 7), B (50-60%, Fair, Grade Point 6), and U (Below 50%, Re-appear, Grade Point 0).

SECTION 3. SEMESTER EXAMINATION ELIGIBILITY
3.1 A student shall be issued an examination hall ticket only if they satisfy the minimum attendance threshold (75% or sanctioned condonation) and have cleared all outstanding college tuition and laboratory dues.
3.2 Malpractice during examinations results in immediate expulsion from the exam hall and cancellation of all theory examinations for the semester.
