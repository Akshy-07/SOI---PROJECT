COLLEGE FEE PAYMENT AND SCHOLARSHIP POLICY 2025-2026

SECTION 1. TUITION AND EXAMINATION FEE SCHEDULE
1.1 Due Date: All semester tuition and examination fees must be remitted on or before October 15 for odd semesters, and March 15 for even semesters.
1.2 Late Fee Surcharge: Remittances made after the designated due date incur a mandatory late surcharge of Rs. 500 up to 15 days, and Rs. 1,500 thereafter up to 30 days.
1.3 Exam Clearance: Students with unpaid tuition or laboratory fees are not permitted to register for semester end examinations.

SECTION 2. FEE REFUND POLICY
2.1 Full or partial refund requests follow official UGC guidelines: 100% refund (minus Rs. 1,000 processing fee) if withdrawal is 15 days or more before the formally notified last date of admission; 80% if less than 15 days before; 50% if within 15 days after; 0% if more than 30 days after the last date.

SECTION 3. MERIT SCHOLARSHIPS
3.1 Students achieving a CGPA of 9.0 or above with zero arrears are awarded a 25% tuition fee waiver for the subsequent academic term.
