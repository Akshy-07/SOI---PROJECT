COLLEGE CAMPUS FACILITIES AND LIBRARY HANDBOOK 2025-2026

SECTION 1. CENTRAL LIBRARY GUIDELINES
1.1 Working Hours: The Central Library operates from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on all working days (Monday to Friday). On Saturdays, working hours are from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. The library remains closed on Sundays and national public holidays.
1.2 Book Borrowing Privileges: Undergraduate students are eligible to borrow up to 3 books simultaneously for a maximum duration of 14 calendar days. Books may be renewed once if no reservation is pending.
1.3 Overdue Fines: A late return fine of Rs. 2 per day per book is levied after the due date. For loss of book, the student must replace the book or pay twice the publisher's price.
1.4 Digital Library: Digital access to IEEE, Springer, and ScienceDirect journals is accessible on campus Wi-Fi via institutional login credentials.

SECTION 2. CAMPUS CODE OF CONDUCT AND DRESS CODE
2.1 Dress Code: All students must adhere to formal or semi-formal attire with college identity card displayed visibly at all times on campus. Sleeveless shirts, round-neck printed t-shirts, and ripped jeans are strictly prohibited.
2.2 Mobile Phone Usage: Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode inside classrooms, laboratories, and the library. Use during lectures without instructor authorization invites disciplinary warning.
2.3 Anti-Ragging Policy: Zero-tolerance policy towards ragging in any form as per UGC and Supreme Court mandates. Violation leads to immediate suspension and criminal FIR.

SECTION 3. HOSTEL REGULATIONS
3.1 In-time Curfew: All hostel residents must return to campus and sign the hostel register by 8:30 PM on weekdays and 9:00 PM on weekends.
3.2 Night Out Permission: Night outs require digital permission slip approved by parents through the student portal at least 24 hours in advance.
