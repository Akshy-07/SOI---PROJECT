COLLEGE ACADEMIC REGULATIONS 2024-2025 (OUTDATED PREVIOUS YEAR)

SECTION 1. ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS
1.1 Every student is required to attend a minimum of 70% of scheduled lectures in each registered course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations.
1.2 Students securing attendance between 60% and 69% may apply for medical condonation upon payment of Rs. 500.
1.3 Students below 60% attendance will be detained.
